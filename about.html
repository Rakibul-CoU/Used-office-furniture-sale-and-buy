Since Day One

Doha's office furniture, kept in circulation.

We started on C Ring Rd with a simple idea: offices open and close every week in Qatar, and the furniture inside them is usually still good. Instead of it ending up in a landfill, we buy it, inspect it, and put it back to work in someone else's office — at a fair price for everyone.

Today we're a 5.0-star rated dealer with 46 reviews, serving businesses across Doha, Al Rayyan, Al Wakrah and beyond — from single-chair orders to full office relocations.
